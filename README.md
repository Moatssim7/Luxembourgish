# 🇱🇺 Learning Luxembourgish & passing the Sproochentest

**Resource guide** · exam prep, courses, books, tools, games and media · *updated November 2026*

> ### How to use this guide
> - **Speaking test (A2):** learn the possible interview and photo topics ([sections 2](#interview)–[3](#photos)), practise with [section 5](#prep), take a course ([section 6](#courses)) and speak at a language café ([section 8](#cafes)).
> - **Listening test (B1):** see what the 3 recordings are usually about ([section 4](#listening)), then listen a little every day – start with Poterkëscht and De Journal ([sections 13](#tv) and [14](#podcasts)).
> - **Every day:** look words up in LOD.lu, hear them with Sproochmaschinn ([section 9](#tools)) and play Wuertle ([section 11](#games)).

## 2. Possible interview topics (not official)

*The INLL publishes no topic list. These come from test-takers and courses. ★ = example interview published by the INLL.*

| Usually taught at | Topics |
|---|---|
| **A1.1** | Sproochen (Languages) · Aarbecht (Work) · Transportmëttelen (Transport) · Heemechtsland & Tourismus (Home country & tourism) |
| **A1.2** | Joreszäiten & Wieder (Seasons & weather) · Hobbyen a Kreativitéit (Hobbies & creativity) · Sport · Akafen (Shopping) · Iessen (Food & eating habits) · Famill (Family) · Sozial Kontakter (Friends & social life) |
| **A2.1** | Wunnen & Uertschaft (Housing & where you live) · De Stot maachen (Housework) · Alldag (Daily life) · Moud a Kleeder (Clothes & fashion) |
| **A2.2** | Kaddoen (Gifts) · **Fester (Festivals & celebrations)** ★ · Vakanz a Reesen (Holidays & travel) · Medien an Technologien (Media & technology) · **Film an Televisioun (Film & TV)** ★ · Gesondheet (Health) · **Schlof an Entspanung (Sleep & relaxation)** ★ · Liesen (Reading) |
| **Less common** | Schoul (School) · Déieren (Animals) · Kandheet an Erënnerungen (Childhood & memories) |

> 💡 **Tip:** In the exam every topic is tested at A2. All official examples follow the same order: general questions → the past → your situation now → your opinion → your plans. For each topic, prepare one short story, your habits, one opinion and one plan.

<a id="photos"></a>

## 3. Possible photo topics (not official)

*You choose 1 of 3 photos of people. These are scenes reported by test-takers and used in courses. ★ = example photo published by the INLL.*

| Theme | Typical scenes |
|---|---|
| **Family & children** | **Family holiday on the beach** ★ · family meal · family in the kitchen · grandparents at home · children at kindergarten · birthday party |
| **Markets & shopping** | **Open-air market** ★ · flower market · flea market · Christmas market · busy shopping street |
| **Town & streets** | Town square · pedestrian zone · café terrace · busy street on a holiday · city at night in the rain |
| **Food & home** | Friends cooking together · restaurant · family dinner · living room and kitchen |
| **Leisure & sport** | Picnic in the park · cycling in the countryside · children playing football · funfair (Schueberfouer) · boat trip |
| **Travel & traffic** | Traffic jam · airport check-in · queue at the airport · train or bus |
| **Events & culture** | Festivals and national day · exhibition · official ceremony |

> 💡 **Tip:** Talk for about 5 minutes: who, where, when (season, weather, time of day), what they are doing, clothes, objects and colours – then guess what happens next and give a short opinion.

<a id="listening"></a>

## 4. Possible listening topics (not official)

*The official format has 3 recordings: a radio message, a conversation on an everyday topic, and an exchange or presentation on a specific topic. The topics below are typical examples.*

| Recording | What it is often about |
|---|---|
| **Part 1 – Radio message** | A short news item: an accident or traffic jam, a police operation, an animal escaped from the zoo, the weather, a local event or an announcement |
| **Part 2 – Everyday conversation** | Two people talking about daily life: making plans or an appointment, shopping, holidays, work, moving house, health |
| **Part 3 – Interview or presentation** | Someone talks about a specific topic: their job, a hobby or club, a festival or tradition, a project, the environment |

> 💡 **Tip:** About 16 multiple-choice questions (A, B or C) on a tablet. Read the questions before each recording starts. Practise with the INLL sample test, Poterkëscht and De Journal.
